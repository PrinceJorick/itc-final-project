    Answer with STAR

    For Tell me about a time… questions, tell a short story in four parts:

    Situation
    Where were you? A class, org, or OJT.
    Task
    What did you need to do?
    Action
    What did you do? Spend the most time here.
    Result
    What changed? Use a number if you can.

    Keep it under two minutes. Prepare three or four stories you can reuse.

    Four questions to prepare first

    They come up in most OJT, BPO, and entry-level interviews.

    Tell me about yourself.

    One minute: what you study, one achievement that fits the job, and why you applied.

    Why should we hire you?

    Match two or three strengths to the job post, each with a quick example.

    What's your expected salary?

    Research the range for the role in your city, then give a range. For OJT, ask about the allowance.

    Can you work night shift?

    Common in BPO. Answer honestly; if yes, show you've planned your commute and sleep.

    What to wear and bring

    Dress one step more formal than the office. If unsure, ask HR about the dress code.

    • Office: collared shirt or blouse, slacks or a knee-length skirt, closed shoes.
    • BPO and tech: smart casual. Polo or blouse, dark pants, closed shoes.
    • Avoid: ripped jeans, shorts, slippers, loud prints, strong perfume.

    Bring in a folder

    • 3–5 printed copies of your resume
    • Two valid IDs and photocopies
    • A black ballpen
    • 2×2 and 1×1 ID photos
    • Documents the employer asked for

    Online interview tips

    Treat a Zoom, Meet, or Teams call like an office visit.

    1. The day before

      Test your camera and mic, charge your laptop, and keep mobile data as a backup.

    2. Your setup

      Face a light source, use a plain background, and raise the camera to eye level.

    3. Ten minutes before

      Close other apps, mute notifications, and keep your resume open on the side.

    4. During the call

      Look at the camera when you speak. If the call drops, rejoin right away.

    5. After the call

      Send a short thank-you email the same day.
