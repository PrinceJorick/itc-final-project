Sign in

An account is optional here — but if this copy has one, signing in keeps the door open for your work to follow you between devices.

Email and password, nothing else

Signing in takes an email address and a password you choose. No profiles to fill out, nothing to post, nothing to share.

Everything on Simula already works without an account: your resume draft, your checklist progress, and the interview cards you write all stay on this device either way.

Forgot your password?

Use the same email and password on every device — with just one field pair, the way in stays simple: create the account again only if you truly lose it.

Your email is used only for signing in. It is never displayed publicly or passed to anyone else.

Your account

Accounts aren't switched on here

This copy of the site runs entirely from its files, so sign-in hasn't been connected to an account service. Everything else works as usual — and stays on your device.
