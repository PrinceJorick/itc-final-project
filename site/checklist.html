RA 11261: the first-time jobseeker's free pass

The First Time Jobseekers Assistance Act (Republic Act No. 11261, signed April 10, 2019) sets aside government fees for the documents your first application needs.

What it covers (Section 4)

  • Police clearance
  • NBI clearance
  • Barangay clearance
  • Medical certificate from a public hospital (lab tests and similar procedures stay paid)
  • Birth certificate
  • Marriage certificate
  • Transcript of records from state colleges and universities
  • Tax Identification Number (TIN)
  • Unified Multi-Purpose ID (UMID) card
  • Other government papers an employer requires of an applicant

How to claim it

  1. Ask your barangay for a certification that you're a first-time jobseeker (Section 5). You'll also sign an Oath of Undertaking there.
  2. Present that certification each time you request a document.

The law lets you use the benefit only once (Section 3), so collect everything you might need while the certification is valid.

What it doesn't cover (Section 8)

  • PRC licensure exam fees
  • DFA passport and authentication ("red ribbon") services
  • Civil Service Commission exams
  • LTO driver's license applications

Ask your barangay first

Particulars — how long you must have lived in the barangay, how long the certification holds — are set by the implementing rules and vary in practice. Ask at the barangay hall before going. The law in full is on LawPhil.
