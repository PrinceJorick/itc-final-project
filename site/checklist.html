RA 11261: free documents for first-time jobseekers

The First Time Jobseekers Assistance Act (Republic Act No. 11261, approved April 10, 2019) waives government fees for documents you need to apply for your first job.

What's free (Section 4)

  • Police clearance
  • NBI clearance
  • Barangay clearance
  • Medical certificate from a public hospital (lab tests and other procedures are still charged)
  • Birth certificate
  • Marriage certificate
  • Transcript of records from state colleges and universities
  • Tax Identification Number (TIN)
  • Unified Multi-Purpose ID (UMID) card
  • Other government documents employers require from applicants

How to claim it

  1. Get a barangay certification that you are a first-time jobseeker (Section 5). Barangays also ask you to sign an Oath of Undertaking.
  2. Show the certification when you request each document.

The law says the benefit can only be availed of once (Section 3), so get all your documents while your certification is valid.

Not covered (Section 8)

  • PRC professional licensure exam fees
  • DFA passport and authentication ("red ribbon") services
  • Civil Service Commission exams
  • LTO driver's license applications

Check with your barangay

Details like how long you must have lived in the barangay or how long the certification stays valid are set by the implementing rules and can differ in practice. Ask your barangay hall before you go. Read the full law on LawPhil.
