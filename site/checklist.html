RA 11261: free documents for first-time jobseekers

The First Time Jobseekers Assistance Act (approved April 10, 2019) waives government fees for documents you need for your first job.

What's free (Section 4)

  • Police, NBI, and barangay clearances
  • Medical certificate from a public hospital (lab tests not included)
  • Birth and marriage certificates
  • Transcript of records from state colleges and universities
  • TIN and UMID card
  • Other government documents employers require

How to claim it

  1. Get a barangay certification that you're a first-time jobseeker (Section 5) and sign an Oath of Undertaking.
  2. Show it whenever you request a document.

You can use the benefit only once (Section 3), so get everything while your certification is valid.

Not covered (Section 8)

  • PRC licensure exams
  • DFA passport and authentication
  • Civil Service exams
  • LTO driver's licenses

Check with your barangay

Residency and validity rules can vary, so ask at your barangay hall first. Read the full law on LawPhil.
