About Simula

Four first-year Computer Science students at Batangas State University – Alangilan. We built this because drafting our own first resumes was rougher than it needed to be.

What we're trying to do

Help Filipino students spend what they already hold — class projects, org positions, contests — on a resume they're glad to hand over, and walk into the first interview ready.

Everything on the site is free, works without an account, and stays on your device. No information of yours is collected or sent anywhere.

The lines we drew

  • Begin from what students already have.
  • One straight path — Guide, Build, Practice — not a drawer of tools.
  • Local and correct beats global and generic.
  • Free and private by default.
