About OJT to Offer

We're four first-year Computer Science students at Batangas State University – Alangilan. We built this because writing our own first resumes was harder than it should be.

Our mission

Help every Filipino student turn what they already have (school projects, org work, contests) into a resume they're proud of, and walk into their first interview prepared.

Everything here is free, needs no account, and stays on your device. We don't collect or send any of your information.

What we believe

  • Start from what students already have.
  • One path, not a pile of tools: Guide, Build, Practice.
  • Local and accurate beats global and generic.
  • Free and private by default.
