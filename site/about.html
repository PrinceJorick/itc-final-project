About Simula

Simula is Filipino for "beginning." We're four Computer Science students at Batangas State University – Alangilan, and we built this to make first applications easier.

Our mission

Help Filipino students turn class projects, org work, and contests into a resume they're proud of, and walk into their first interview prepared.

Everything here is free and works without an account. Your data stays on your device.

What we believe

  • Start from what students already have.
  • One path: Guide, Build, Practice.
  • Local and accurate beats global and generic.
  • Free and private by default.
