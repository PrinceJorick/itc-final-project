## 2. Gaps

### Verified shortcomings (with source)
1. **PDF download is behind a paywall on some popular builders.** Resume.io lists "Unlimited PDF downloads" as a premium perk ([help.resume.io](https://help.resume.io/en/articles/3785856)).
2. **Free plans cap length or count.** Novoresume's free plan is one resume and one page ([novoresume.com/llms.txt](https://novoresume.com/llms.txt)). Jobscan's free plan gives about 5 scans a month ([weekday.works](https://www.weekday.works/post/jobscan-review), third-party).
3. **Student discounts need foreign verification services.** Kickresume's student Premium is verified via ISIC, ITIC, or UNiDAYS ([kickresume.com/students](https://www.kickresume.com/students/)).
4. **Interview practice is separate from resume building.** Google Interview Warmup and Big Interview are interview-only tools; Canva, Novoresume, and Enhancv are resume-only (sources in the table).
5. **PH advice exists but is not interactive.** JobStreet PH publishes OJT and fresh-grad resume advice as articles, without a builder or practice mode ([JobStreet OJT article](https://ph.jobstreet.com/career-advice/article/tips-and-resume-sample-for-ojt-applicants)).
6. **Design-first templates can hurt ATS parsing.** Reviewers note many Canva templates are not ATS-friendly ([rezi.ai](https://www.rezi.ai/posts/canva-ai-resume-builder-review), third-party).

### Assumptions (not verified; to test with classmates)
1. Most global builders assume you have work experience, and their default section order puts "Experience" first. *(Observed on demo templates; not formally measured.)*
2. None of the global tools above explain Philippine conventions such as whether to add a photo, age, or full address, or the pre-employment documents (NBI, SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, TIN, PSA) and RA 11261. *(We found no such content in their product pages, but did not audit every blog post.)*
3. Filipino students are less likely to pay a recurring USD subscription for a resume download. *(Reasonable, but we have no survey data.)*
4. First-year students don't know how to turn school projects and org work into resume bullets. *(Based on team experience.)*

## 3. Final UVP

> **OJT to Offer helps Filipino students turn school projects and org work into a one-page resume, then practice the interview questions local employers actually ask, free, with no sign-up.**

Short form for the hero: *From school projects to your first job offer.*

## 4. Differentiators the site actually delivers

Each one maps to a feature that can be shown in the demo.

| # | Differentiator | Where it shows on the site |
|---|----------------|-----------------------------|
| 1 | **Built for "no experience yet."** A toggle hides the Experience section and moves Projects and Organizations up, and a rule-based bullet helper turns school work into strong bullets. | `builder.html`: "No experience yet" toggle, Bullet helper |
| 2 | **Resume and interview in one path.** Guide → Build → Practice, plus a job-fit checker that compares your resume skills with a real OJT or job posting. | `index.html` journey, `builder.html` job-fit checker, `interview.html` |
| 3 | **Philippines-specific content.** Local resume conventions, common Filipino interview questions ("Expected salary?"), and a pre-employment checklist with RA 11261 free-document info, cited to government sources. | `resume-guide.html`, `interview.html`, `checklist.html` |
| 4 | **Free, no account, private.** No sign-up, no paywall on PDF download, and your data stays in your own browser (localStorage). Works offline. | Whole site; `builder.html` Download PDF |
| 5 | **Pre-employment checklist that remembers your progress.** Checkboxes, a progress bar, and "how to get it" steps for each document. | `checklist.html` |

We do not claim to be the "first" or "only" tool of this kind.
